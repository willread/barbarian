Steel. Blood. Thunder.

A dark fantasy arcade brawler. Take up your axe, break the enemy line, and carve your way through a world of ruined stone and old magic.

Close the distance. Commit to the swing. When the horde closes in, call down the storm.

Leave nothing standing

  • Heavy steel. Axe and sword combat with running charges, spinning attacks, and strikes from the air.
  • Old magic. Unleash lightning when your magic is ready and turn a crowded battlefield into ash.
  • A world in ruins. Fight through crumbling strongholds, haunted wetlands, and fire-scarred lands.

Take up arms

Play with a keyboard and mouse or a controller.

Move — WASD / arrows
Attack — J / left mouse
Jump — Space / K
Magic — L / right mouse
Run — Shift
Pause — Escape

Controller: left stick or D-pad to move, X / Square to attack, A / Cross to jump, Y / Triangle for magic, and RB / R1 to run.

Contains fantasy violence and blood.
