# Final cutscene music concept: Homeward

Keep the existing burning-fortress-to-sunset panorama. The music follows its
shift from destruction to reunion. A breathy, human pan flute carries a short,
memorable melody over sparse fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a low warm drone.
No triumphant metal finale: the revenge is over, and his goat is back.

- 0–5 seconds, burning fortress: one fading low guitar resonance, quiet wind,
  then a single distant pan-flute note. Lots of space.
- 5–12 seconds, the road: a tentative three-note flute phrase; soft acoustic
  guitar joins with widely spaced plucked notes. No busy percussion.
- 12–18 seconds, Cairn and the goat: repeat the phrase with a warmer answering
  note and an open, hopeful chord. A little breath and finger noise keep it human.
- 18.5 seconds, "I missed you, kid": flute rests and accompaniment drops beneath
  the voice. Let the line land plainly; no comedy sting.
- 20–22 seconds, sunset: one gentle answering flute note and a resolving guitar
  chord, with a short natural tail into the fade.

Tone: weathered, tender sword-and-sorcery folk. Approximately 65–70 BPM, flexible
phrasing, minor/modal beginning resolving toward warmth. Avoid a jaunty village
tune, sweeping orchestra, or glossy spa-music production.
