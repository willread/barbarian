# Shareware upgrade screen

## Direction

A classic PC shareware end screen interpreted through Cairn's existing stone,
fire and painted dark-fantasy art. A large, direct invitation to purchase, a
brief reminder of the quest, and two glimpses of the remaining episodes.

**BUY THE FULL GAME**

REGISTER YOUR COPY. COMPLETE THE QUEST.

## Layout and behavior

Use the 1440×810 game composition. Copy and two episode illustrations occupy
the left portion; Cairn and firelit ruins dominate the right. Chunky stone
lettering and a hewn stone purchase button match the existing menus. Use the
existing warm serif typography for supporting copy.

Suggested motion is restrained: glowing button selection, drifting embers,
flickering fire. Introduce the screen with the existing stone-drop language,
only after any prior cutscene finishes. Keep Back immediately available, with
keyboard, controller and mouse navigation and no forced countdown.

No price or store URL is assumed. An implemented purchase button should use
the eventual actual store destination. This is a design mockup, not a wired
purchase flow or a change to episode access.

## Artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using
`godot/art/title-background.png` as the visual identity reference. Prompt
direction: a complete 16:9 shareware upgrade screen, large chiseled Cairn logo,
flat charcoal stone behind readable copy, realistic painted Cairn in firelit
ruins, swamp and infernal foundry previews, parchment-colored serif copy,
oversized stone Buy button and smaller Back action. Homage to early PC
shareware registration screens, without invented prices or contact details.
