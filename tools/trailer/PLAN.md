# Cairn trailer plan — 56 seconds

## Reference and scope

Reference: https://store.steampowered.com/app/588650/Dead_Cells/

The single 56-second Steam trailer was visually inspected in the browser, including seeking and playback. Observed elements: moonlit opening; early red award laurels; combat and traversal across contrasting environments; recurring red review/score cards on a dark atmospheric background; gameplay without the normal persistent HUD, while damage/status feedback remains; a giant boss threat around 51 seconds; an Available Now/platform card around 53 seconds. Sampled timings are approximate, not a frame-accurate edit decision list. Browser screenshots do not provide an audio audition, so no claims about the reference's music, exact beat sync, or mix are made.

Interpretation: repeated bursts of visual evidence separated by short punctuation make variety easy to absorb. Strong color/location changes, readable attack silhouettes, and an escalation in threat carry the visual sales pitch. Borrow that structure with Cairn's own identity. Do not imitate unearned review quotes or awards.

## Creative direction

Heavy, physical dark-fantasy arcade combat. Use three concise chapter words from the existing tagline: STEEL / BLOOD / THUNDER. Give each a brief full-frame treatment using existing Cairn typography, stone, blood and ember motifs. Approximately 47.5 seconds of gameplay, 3.5 seconds of interstitials, and 5 seconds of branding/CTA. No opening studio splash or story exposition.

| Time | Picture / required action | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Axe dive lands with a clear knockdown and visible aftermath in the aqueduct. Start just before contact, not after it. | Immediate understandable hook. |
| 3–4 | STEEL | First punctuation. |
| 4–11 | Axe combination, sword exchange, charge; distribute across citadel, swamp and ashen scenery. Approximately 2–3 seconds each. | Core feel and all three worlds early. |
| 11–15 | Warden attack tell, player avoids it and punishes the recovery. | Early boss tease and evidence of skill. |
| 15–16 | BLOOD | Reset attention. |
| 16–24 | Spin breaks pressure; shield opening punished; successful combination builds multiplier. Show real HUD for the scoring shot. | Combat choices and mastery. |
| 24–30 | Mire avoidance, then a bomb struck back into its sender. Give the reflection a continuous 3–4-second setup/action/payoff. | Distinctive interactions. |
| 30–31.5 | THUNDER | Set up the biggest effects. |
| 31.5–38 | Lightning payoff; earned weapon in action; King root/charge encounter. | Power, progression and changing threats. |
| 38–45 | Saint furnace attack or volley: readable tell and player response, followed by escalating combat cuts. | Climactic danger. |
| 45–49 | Four short successful action payoffs across different locations, about one second each. | Final acceleration. |
| 49–51 | Most imposing boss attack fills the composition; cut on the attack before resolving the encounter. | Leave a challenge to anticipate. |
| 51–56 | CAIRN, STEEL. BLOOD. THUNDER., one confirmed CTA. | Name retention and next action. |

Target 18–22 selected gameplay shots. Exact boundaries adapt to music and the captured actions; final duration remains below 60 seconds. World variety is interwoven, not isolated in sightseeing segments. Bosses recur through the edit rather than getting three equal consecutive blocks. Do not show boss deaths or the ending.

## Capture package

- Extend the existing offline Godot trailer staging to cover all three episodes and current mechanics. The old script and edit are a foundation, not final footage.
- Capture 2–3 takes for each key action, including 1–2 seconds of handles before and after. Select by clarity of intent, successful contact, readable reaction and attractive composition.
- Keep real gameplay speed, damage, enemy behavior and plausible encounter sizes. Stage positions, seeds and earned loadouts as needed. Do not rely on frozen opponents or universally weakened enemies to imply false combat pacing.
- Add capture-only HUD visibility control. Use clean gameplay for most shots, retaining in-world effects and tells. Include a normal-HUD combo shot. Hiding HUD must not stretch the arena or reveal unfinished scenery; inspect framing first.
- Target native 1920×1080, 60 fps after validating offline capture against simulation timing. Existing captures are 30 fps. Do not interpolate a 30 fps recording to claim 60 fps.
- Separate game SFX from music; retain useful voice moments only if they strengthen a shot. Keep recordings and renders on E:/Cairn-build-tools/trailer. No new Windows executable location.
- Confirm the TODO about episode 3 screen 4 animation is current before selecting that environment for a hero shot. Other game work may be in progress; capture from an agreed stable revision.

## Editing and sound

Use hard cuts and a consistent visual location for the hero where practical. Choose cuts around contacts and completed reactions. Preserve enough setup for bomb reflection and boss tells. Avoid constant zooms, black bars, artificial speedups or unrelated cinematic footage.

Audition the existing guitar music first. Make a deliberate 56-second arrangement with an opening accent, space at cards, a growing final section and a resolved ending. Duck music around hero impacts; allow a brief drop before the final threat/title. These are production recommendations, not verified observations about the reference audio. Use existing/licensed assets; no purchase is required to make the rough cut.

## Review and delivery

1. Capture shortlist and contact sheet: choose strong moments before polishing.
2. Full 56-second rough cut with provisional title cards, music and game SFX.
3. Review muted at small store-player size: genre, actions, variety and name must read clearly. Listen separately for clipped or masked impacts and an abrupt music ending.
4. Polish timing, sound and title motion; inspect the complete export for dropped frames, HUD artifacts, cropping and truthful feature depiction.
5. Deliver a local H.264/AAC MP4 and reusable edit/capture sources. Do not publish.

## User input

No manual playthrough recording is required initially. The existing capture pipeline can stage the shots. The only unresolved marketing input is the CTA and destination: wishlist, demo/shareware, or full-game availability. Use a clearly marked CTA placeholder in the rough cut until confirmed. User review of the first full cut should focus on combat feel, music and any spoiler concerns. This document is planning only; no new footage or game changes have been made for it.
