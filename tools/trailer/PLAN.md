## Reference and scope

Reference: https://store.steampowered.com/app/588650/Dead_Cells/

The single 56-second Steam trailer was visually inspected in the browser, including seeking and playback. Observed elements: moonlit opening; early red award laurels; combat and traversal across contrasting environments; recurring red review/score cards on a dark atmospheric background; gameplay without the normal persistent HUD, while damage/status feedback remains; a giant boss threat around 51 seconds; an Available Now/platform card around 53 seconds. Sampled timings are approximate, not a frame-accurate edit decision list. Browser screenshots do not provide an audio audition, so no claims about the reference's music, exact beat sync, or mix are made.

Interpretation: repeated bursts of visual evidence separated by short punctuation make variety easy to absorb. Strong color/location changes, readable attack silhouettes, and an escalation in threat carry the visual sales pitch. Borrow that structure with Cairn's own identity. Do not imitate unearned review quotes or awards.

## Creative direction

Heavy, physical dark-fantasy arcade combat. Use three concise chapter words from the existing tagline: STEEL / BLOOD / THUNDER. Give each a brief full-frame treatment using existing Cairn typography, stone, blood and ember motifs. Approximately 47.5 seconds of gameplay, 3.5 seconds of interstitials, and 5 seconds of branding/CTA. No opening studio splash or story exposition.

## Capture package

- Capture continuous manual runs through all three episodes using OBS. The old staged capture and edit remain reference material, not the source of authentic new gameplay footage.
- Capture 2–3 takes for each key action, including 1–2 seconds of handles before and after. Select by clarity of intent, successful contact, readable reaction and attractive composition.
- Keep real gameplay speed, damage, enemy behavior, earned equipment and naturally accumulated combat state. Do not assign score, multipliers, mana or blood for a shot, freeze opponents or weaken enemies for trailer pacing.
- Keep the full normal HUD throughout. Preserve battlefield and HUD continuity within each exchange; cuts between different runs and locations are fine.
- Target native 1920×1080, 60 fps after validating offline capture against simulation timing. Existing captures are 30 fps. Do not interpolate a 30 fps recording to claim 60 fps.
- Separate game SFX from music; retain useful voice moments only if they strengthen a shot. Keep recordings and renders on E:/Cairn-build-tools/trailer. No new Windows executable location.
- Confirm the TODO about episode 3 screen 4 animation is current before selecting that environment for a hero shot. Other game work may be in progress; capture from an agreed stable revision.

## Editing and sound

Use hard cuts and a consistent visual location for the hero where practical. Choose cuts around contacts and completed reactions. Preserve enough setup for bomb reflection and boss tells. Avoid constant zooms, black bars, artificial speedups or unrelated cinematic footage.

Audition the existing guitar music first. Make a deliberate 56-second arrangement with an opening accent, space at cards, a growing final section and a resolved ending. Duck music around hero impacts; allow a brief drop before the final threat/title. These are production recommendations, not verified observations about the reference audio. Use existing/licensed assets; no purchase is required to make the rough cut.

5. Deliver a local H.264/AAC MP4 and reusable edit/capture sources. Do not publish.
